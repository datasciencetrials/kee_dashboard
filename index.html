Ministry of Works and Transport: Department of National Roads oversees 21,284.02 km of National Roads (6,406.69 km paved and 14,877.33 km unpaved), 144.56 km of Expressways, 552 bridges, and 452 major culverts.

1. View-Only Architecture & Scope

The KEE ITS Dashboard is a strictly view-only analytical portal operated by the Ministry of Works and Transport (MoWT), Department of National Roads. It provides real-time corridor monitoring, historic trends, and Vision 2040 projections without user data entry or administrative access controls.

  • Corridor coverage: Kampala to Entebbe Expressway (KEE Mainline) and Munyonyo Spur.
  • Primary monitoring stations: Busega, Kajjansi, and Mpala.
  • All analytics support PNG and CSV downloads.

2. Authoritative Network & Structure Baselines

  • National Road Network: 21,284.02 km total extent (6,406.69 km paved, 30.10%; 14,877.33 km unpaved, 69.90%).
  • Functional Classes: International Trunk Roads (2,604.65 km), National Trunk Roads (2,845.27 km), Primary Roads (15,689.54 km), and Expressways (144.56 km).
  • Bridges: 552 structures (Good 247, Satisfactory 71, Marginal 162, Poor 52, Critical 13, Under Construction 7). Key monitored structure: B162 Atari Bridge (Muyembe to Namalu, Eastern Region).
  • Major Culverts: 452 structures (Good 403, Satisfactory 39, Marginal 4, Poor 1, Critical 4, Under Construction 1).

3. Mandatory Survey Tool Standards

Pavement and corridor condition assessments integrated into this platform use the Ministry's standardised equipment:

  • MOBICAP(Road condition assesment tool): Mobile visual and geometric condition capture along paved and unpaved corridors.
  • ROMDAS(Roughness and rutting machine based testing): Machine-based International Roughness Index (IRI) and rut depth measurements.

4. Vision 2040 Projection & Data Standards

  • Vision 2040 Alignment: Traffic demand (AADT) and toll revenue trajectories are projected to 2040 using an annual corridor growth factor of 3.2% to 4.5%.
  • Date Standard: All timestamps and registers strictly follow dd/mm/yyyy formatting.
  • Units & Coordinates: Metric units (km, m, mm, T, km/h) and geographic coordinates in decimal degrees.
  • Reporting Discipline: Road extent is strictly expressed in kilometres (km) and percentages (%).
